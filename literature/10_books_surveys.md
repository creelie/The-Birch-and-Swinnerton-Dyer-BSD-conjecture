# 10. Books, lecture notes and surveys

## 10.1 Textbooks

| Reference | Level and use |
|---|---|
| SilvermanTate2015 | Undergraduate introduction: rational points, Nagell–Lutz, Mordell's theorem, complex multiplication. |
| Silverman2009 | The standard graduate text; Chapter X (descent, Selmer and $Ш$), Appendix C.16 states BSD. |
| Husemoller2004 | Graduate text through $L$-functions and the conjecture; appendices by Otto Forster, Ruth Lawrence and Stefan Theisen. |
| Cremona1997book | Algorithms for modular elliptic curves: modular symbols, periods, $L$-values, the tables. |
| Darmon2004 | Heegner points, Gross–Zagier and Kolyvagin, Stark–Heegner points; CBMS notes. |
| DarmonZhang2004 | MSRI volume on Heegner points and Rankin $L$-series. |
| Rubin2000 | Euler systems in general; Kolyvagin and Kato as instances. |
| YuanZhangZhang2013 | The Gross–Zagier formula on Shimura curves in full generality (Annals of Math. Studies 184). |

## 10.3 Founding papers

BirchSwinnertonDyer1963, BirchSwinnertonDyer1965 (the conjecture and the computations behind
it), Cassels1962, Cassels1965 (the Cassels–Tate pairing and isogeny invariance), Tate1966 (the
general statement and the function-field analogue), and the BSD chapters of Tate1974.
