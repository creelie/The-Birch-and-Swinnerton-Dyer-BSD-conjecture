# 5. Ranks on average, distributions and heuristics

## 5.1 Averages over all curves (ordered by naive height)

* Brumer1992, HeathBrown2004, Young2006: under GRH, average analytic rank
  bounded by 2.3, 2, 25/14 respectively.
* BhargavaShankar2015a/2015b and the 4-, 5-Selmer preprints
  (BhargavaShankar4Sel, BhargavaShankar5Sel): average size of $\operatorname{Sel}_n$
  equals $\sigma(n)$ for $n=2,3,4,5$; the average rank is less than $0.885$;
  a positive proportion of curves has rank 0. BhargavaSkinner2014: a positive
  proportion has rank 1.
* BhargavaSkinnerZhang2014: more than 66% of all curves satisfy the rank part of BSD
  (and have finite $Ш$); a positive proportion satisfy the full $p$-part for many $p$.
* Second moment of $\#\operatorname{Sel}_2$ (Bhargava–Shankar–Swaminathan, arXiv 2110.09063).

## 5.2 Quadratic twist families and Goldfeld's conjecture

Goldfeld's conjecture (Goldfeld1979): in the twist family of a fixed curve,
50% of twists have rank 0 and 50% rank 1, and the average rank is $1/2$.

* HeathBrown1994, SwinnertonDyer2008, Kane2013: distribution of 2-Selmer ranks in twist families.
* Smith2017, Smith2025 (and arXiv 2207.05674): $2^\infty$- and $\ell^\infty$-Selmer distributions;
  for curves with full rational 2-torsion and no cyclic 4-isogeny, 100% of twists have
  2-Selmer corank $\le1$. Combined with the $p$-converse theorems this gives the odd-parity half
  of Goldfeld for many families.
* KrizLi2019: Goldfeld for a large class of curves in the odd/even sense, positive proportions via
  congruences of Heegner points. BurungaleTian2021, BurungaleTian2025: the even-parity Goldfeld
  conjecture for the congruent number family.
* CoatesLiTianZhai2015, BKLOS2019: positive proportions of rank 0 or 1 twists.
* The full Goldfeld conjecture for every curve is claimed in unrefereed 2026 preprints
  (OpenAI2026Goldfeld, OpenAI2026MeanRank); see §11.

## 5.3 Heuristics

Delaunay2001 (Cohen–Lenstra for $Ш$), Watkins2008, PoonenRains2012
(maximal isotropic subspaces), BKLPR2015 (joint model for ranks, Selmer groups
and $Ш$), ParkPoonenVoightWood2019 (ranks bounded: only finitely many curves of rank $>21$).
The current rank records (§7.4) do not contradict this heuristic, which is about
all but finitely many curves.

## 5.4 Rank records

From Dujella's table (Dujella, consulted 3 Nov 2026): rank $\ge28$ Elkies (2006;
exactly 28 under GRH, KlagsbrunShermanWeigandt2019), $\ge29$ Elkies–Klagsbrun (2024),
$\ge30$ and $\ge31$ Alpöge–Howell (2026), $\ge32$ Elkies–Klagsbrun–Li (2026). The largest
rank known unconditionally and exactly is 22 (S. Li, 2026). Over function fields ranks are
unbounded (Ulmer2002).
